# Loft Japanese (ja) glossary

Address: polite です/ます in sentences, no honorific excess (no 〜させていただきます, no ご〜くださいませ). Avoid
「あなた」 where Japanese leaves the subject out; use it only where the contrast needs it (「あなたのゲートウェイ」 vs
someone else's). Buttons and menu items: short dictionary-form verbs or nouns (続ける, キャンセル, 完了, 作成), as in
Apple/Google/Microsoft Japanese UI. Headings and titles: no 。 unless the English heading is a full sentence used as
a slogan (hero lines, h1 statements keep 。).

Typography: full-width punctuation 。、：？！（）; quotes 「」 (『』 inside 「」); ellipsis "…" (one character, no space
before it: 「手動で設定…」); path separator "›" and arrows "→" kept as in English; the "·" separator in tray/status
lines is kept with spaces (" · ") as a layout glyph. No space between Japanese and Latin words or digits in running
text (Apple Japanese style: 「NASのユーザー名」「Loftゲートウェイ」「30日間」). Exception: in plain-text app strings a
half-width space on both sides of a command (「ゲートウェイで loft-gateway pair を実行します」) so the command stays
readable; inside HTML the <code> tag already sets it off, so no spaces there.

Katakana long vowels: ユーザー, サーバー, フォルダ, プロバイダ (as Google Japanese writes them), used the same way on every
platform so the site and all apps match.

Plurals: Japanese has no grammatical plural; "one" and "other" (and Windows .one/.other) carry the same text.

## Tagline and recurring lines

| English | Japanese |
|---|---|
| Not the cloud. The loft. | クラウドではなく、ロフトに。 |
| Your files, kept at home. | ファイルは自宅に置いたまま。 |
| Both treasury and best parlour. | 宝物庫であり、最上の客間でもある。 |

The tagline: in Japanese ロフト is the everyday word for a loft/attic space (屋根裏), so the product name Loft and the
room ロフト line up the same way as in English, and クラウド is the everyday word for the cloud. 「ロフトに」 (in the
loft) answers the implied "where are your files kept"; it keeps the contrast without adding a claim.

## Other decisions

- Bold lead-ins in website paragraphs end in 「：」 (「<strong>パスワード：</strong>」) or a full-width ？ when the English is a
  question; never an English period.
- App Store keywords leave out ファイル and 自宅 because they are already in the subtitle (Apple indexes the subtitle).
- `web:pages.privacy.sections[5].body` ends 「お問い合わせ：hello@incremental.no.」 with an ASCII period: the tool's URL check
  counts the sentence's final period as part of the host ("incremental.no."), so it must stay. Rewrite without the
  period if the tool's URL regex is ever fixed.
