# Loft glossary: Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR)

Address the user with *você* (possessive *seu/sua*, never *teu/tua*). Brazilian spelling and vocabulary throughout
(celular, arquivo, tela, tentar novamente, contêiner), never European Portuguese (telemóvel, ficheiro, ecrã).

- Buttons and menu items: **infinitive**, short, as Apple, Google and Microsoft do in pt-BR ("Parear", "Entrar",
  "Cancelar", "Continuar", "Criar", "Renomear", "Excluir", "Colar").
- Instructions inside sentences: imperative with *você* ("Execute", "Abra", "Digite", "Verifique", "Tente
  novamente", "Toque", "Puxe para baixo").
- Sentence case. Quotes: “…” (as in the English and in Apple/Google pt-BR). "…" is one character, no space before
  it. Norwegian «…» in source titles stay as they are (they are Norwegian titles).
- Articles with names: *o Loft* (m., "abra o Loft", "do Loft", "no Loft"), *o NAS* (m.), *o gateway* (m.),
  *o app* (m.), *o Finder*, *o iPhone*, *o Mac*, *o PC*.

## Tagline and recurring lines

- "Not the cloud. The loft." → **"Não é a nuvem. É o sótão."** In Brazil *loft* means an open-plan apartment, so
  the name itself cannot carry the pun; *sótão* (the room under the roof) keeps the contrast between the sky and a
  room under your own roof, which is what the about page explains.
- "Your files, kept at home." → "Seus arquivos, guardados em casa." (App Store subtitle, 30-char limit: "Seus
  arquivos ficam em casa")
- "Free and open source" → "Gratuito e de código aberto"
- "Loft works from anywhere" → "o Loft funciona de qualquer lugar"
- "Something went wrong. Try again." → "Algo deu errado. Tente novamente."
- "Too many tries. Wait 15 minutes, then try again." → "Muitas tentativas. Aguarde 15 minutos e tente novamente."
- "Cancel" → "Cancelar"; "Continue" → "Continuar"; "Back" → "Voltar"; "Create" → "Criar"; "Paste" → "Colar"
- "No one else stores your files." → "Ninguém mais guarda seus arquivos."
- "The NAS stays in charge." → "Quem manda é o NAS."
- "whoever runs the NAS" → "quem administra o NAS"
