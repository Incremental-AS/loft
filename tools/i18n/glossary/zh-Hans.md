# Loft Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans) glossary

Address: 你 (never 您), as current Apple/Google/Microsoft zh-CN UI does. Short, plain, calm; no marketing adjectives.

Typography (mainland norms, as Apple zh-CN writes it):
- Full-width punctuation in Chinese text: ，。：；？！（）. Quotes “…” (‘…’ inside). The ellipsis is the single
  character "…" (as Apple zh-CN menus write "设置…"), never "..." and not doubled.
- One half-width space between hanzi and Latin letters/digits ("在 NAS 上", "15 分钟", "运行 <code>…</code>"); no
  space next to full-width punctuation or quotes (“文件”App is Apple's own spelling, no space before App).
- The menu path separator "›" keeps a space on each side.
- Placeholders keep their curly quotes: “%@” stays “%@”.
- No full stop at the end of buttons, labels, headings that have none in English; headings that end with "." in
  English (website h1/kickers like "Get Loft.") take "。" only where Chinese headings would; short taglines keep 。.

## Tagline

"Not the cloud. The loft." = "不是云端，是阁楼。" 阁楼 is the ordinary Chinese word for an attic/loft room (and the word
used for "loft" in Chinese generally), so the contrast survives: 云端 (the cloud) vs 阁楼 (the room under your own
roof), two syllables each. The product name stays "Loft" everywhere else.

"Your files, kept at home." = "你的文件，留在家里。" (App Store subtitle without the full stop.)
