# Loft German (de) glossary

Address: *du* (lower case, as in current Apple/Google/Microsoft German UI). Sentence case in UI text.
Typography: „…“ quotes (‚…‘ inside), one-character ellipsis "…" preceded by a space after a word in menu items
and buttons ("Manuell einrichten …", Apple/Duden style), "–" with spaces as dash. No capital after a colon unless
a full sentence or noun follows (German rule, not the Norwegian one).

## Tagline

"Not the cloud. The loft." = „Nicht die Cloud. Das Loft.“ German uses "die Cloud" for the cloud and "das Loft"
is an ordinary German noun for a room/home under the roof, so the name and the contrast both survive.
