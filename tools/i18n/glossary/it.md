# Loft glossary: Italian (it)

Address the user with *tu*. Buttons and menu items: imperative, short ("Abbina", "Accedi", "Annulla", "Continua",
"Fine"). Sentence case. Quotes: “…” (as Apple/Google Italian UIs). "…" is one character, no space before it.

## Tagline and recurring lines

- "Not the cloud. The loft." → **"Non il cloud. La soffitta."** In Italian "loft" means an open-plan
  apartment, not an attic, so the pun cannot be carried by the name itself; "la soffitta" (the attic room) keeps
  the contrast cloud/sky vs. a room under your own roof, which is what the about page explains.
- "Your files, kept at home." → "I tuoi file, a casa tua."
- "Free and open source" → "Gratuito e open source"
- "Loft works from anywhere" → "Loft funziona ovunque"
- "Try again." → "Riprova."; "Something went wrong." → "Si è verificato un problema."
- "Cancel" → "Annulla"; "Continue" → "Continua"; "Done" → "Fine"; "Back" → "Indietro"
